# Design system

- Minimalist: neutral palette plus one accent, generous whitespace, one self-hosted type family, large type.
- Colour, type, spacing, radius and motion come from CSS-variable tokens. Light and dark. No hard-coded values.
- Heatmaps: perceptually uniform, colour-blind-safe colour maps (not red-only), always with a legend and a text alternative.
- Motion is subtle and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- WCAG AA contrast, labelled controls, text alternatives for charts.
- Mobile patterns: bottom sheets, thumb-reachable primary actions, safe areas.
- Copy: plain language, cosmetic framing, in the `content` module.
- Skin-tone-neutral UI: no imagery or colour meaning that assumes a skin tone.
