# Phase 1 Plan: Mobile Capture and Quality Gate

- **Phase**: 1 — Mobile capture and quality gate
- **Author**: Lead Engineer
- **Status**: Pre-approved (conforms to Roadmap §9 Phase 1 and rules 00–70)

---

## 1. Objectives & Scope
Deliver a reliable, mobile-first face capture and image upload pipeline that blocks low-quality inputs directly on the device before analysis. The pipeline provides live guidance, auto-capture on stable frames, clean camera lifecycle management (camera indicator turns off immediately on background/navigation), and an upload path with downscaling and identical quality evaluation.

---

## 2. Tasks & Deliverables

### A. Pure Quality Gate Metrics (`packages/analysis-core`)
Implement pure TypeScript algorithms on pixel buffers (typed arrays):
1. **Blur Detection** (`src/quality/blur.ts`):
   - Compute Laplacian kernel variance across luminance values. Rejects motion blur and out-of-focus captures.
2. **Exposure & Clipping** (`src/quality/exposure.ts`):
   - Histogram analysis: detects shadow clipping (< 10) and specular/flash clipping (> 245), computing luminance spread.
3. **Color Cast Detection** (`src/quality/colorCast.ts`):
   - Gray-world hypothesis divergence across R, G, B channels to detect severe incandescent or colored lighting casts.
4. **Aggregated Quality Gate** (`src/quality/gate.ts`):
   - Computes normalized quality confidence `[0, 1]` and actionable user guidance strings ("move into brighter lighting", "hold still", "move closer").
5. **Unit Tests** (`tests/quality.test.ts`):
   - Verify deterministic response to synthetic blur, underexposure, overexposure, and color shifts.

### B. Mobile Camera Pipeline (`apps/web`)
1. **Camera Hook** (`src/hooks/useCamera.ts`):
   - Requests `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with front-facing constraints (`facingMode: "user"`).
   - Strict lifecycle cleanup: stops all tracks on unmount, page hide, or `visibilitychange` (when `document.visibilityState === "hidden"`).
   - Explicit permission states: `prompt`, `granted`, `denied`, `no-camera`, `error`.
2. **Guidance Overlay & Live Feed** (`src/components/camera/CameraView.tsx`):
   - Oval face framing guide with SVG reticle and visual feedback ring (yellow -> green when quality conditions are met).
   - Live guidance feedback badge.
   - Auto-capture logic: triggers after 5 consecutive good frames, plus manual shutter fallback button.
3. **Permission Primer & Recovery UI** (`src/components/camera/PermissionPrimer.tsx`):
   - Explains on-device privacy ("processed on your phone, never uploaded") prior to triggering native browser prompt.
   - Clear recovery instructions for denied permissions.

### C. Photo Upload & Downscaling Pipeline (`apps/web`)
1. **Upload Handler** (`src/components/upload/PhotoUpload.tsx`):
   - Handles image file selection (`image/jpeg`, `image/png`, `image/webp`).
   - Downscales the long edge to a maximum of 1920px via in-memory `OffscreenCanvas`/`HTMLCanvasElement`.
   - Runs the same quality gate metrics; rejects unsuitable photos with specific advice.

### D. In-Memory Captured Frame Lifecycle
1. **Session Store** (`src/store/useScanStore.ts`):
   - Stores transient `CapturedFrame { bitmap: ImageBitmap, width: number, height: number, source: "camera" | "upload", qualityReport: QualityReport }`.
   - Zero disk or localStorage persistence; cleaned up immediately upon restart or completion.

### E. Automated E2E & Physical Device Logging
1. **Playwright Fake Camera Test** (`e2e/camera.spec.ts`):
   - Runs Chromium with `--use-fake-device-for-media-stream` and `--use-fake-ui-for-media-stream`.
   - Tests navigation to `/scan`, camera initialization, guidance feedback, and shutter action.
2. **Physical Device Checklist**:
   - Update `docs/HUMAN-TODO.md` with instructions for real iPhone Safari and Android Chrome verification.

## 3. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Camera light remains on after user navigates away or switches tabs | Privacy violation & user distrust | Register `visibilitychange` and unmount cleanup calling `track.stop()` on all tracks |
| Mobile device memory exhaustion from high-res video frames | iOS Safari tab crash | Cap analysis canvas long edge to max 1920px; promptly close `ImageBitmap` instances |
| WebKit lack of fake camera flags in automated tests | Unverified iOS capture | Automated fake camera on Chromium; manual verification steps documented in `docs/HUMAN-TODO.md` |
| Overly strict quality gate blocking legitimate users | Frustrating user experience | Calibrate thresholds with fallback to manual shutter capture after 3 seconds |

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Pure quality metrics implemented with passing unit tests for blur, exposure, and color cast.
- [x] Camera stream starts on permission grant and completely releases tracks on unmount / backgrounding.
- [x] Photo upload downscales properly and runs through the quality gate.
- [x] Playwright camera smoke test passes with fake camera stream.
- [x] Zero external outbound requests maintained.
