## 1. What Was Built & Shipped

### Phase 3: Analyzers v1 (Classical CV)
- Implemented pure TypeScript computer vision analyzers in `packages/analysis-core/src/analyzers/`:
  - **Redness (`redness`)**: Measures erythema elevation in CIELAB $a^*$ relative to personal baseline across the skin mask, outputting region-specific scores and normalized UV erythema heatmaps.
  - **Pigmentation (`pigmentation`)**: Tone classification via Individual Typology Angle (ITA° proxy for Fitzpatrick I–VI tones) and localized hyperpigmentation / dark spot detection via Difference-of-Gaussians (DoG) with kernel scaled to physical millimetres.
  - **Oiliness (`oiliness`)**: Detects specular reflections from facial sebum (high luminance $L^*$, low chroma $C^*$) with clinical sensitivity weighting across the T-zone.
  - **Texture / Pores (`texture`)**: 2D discrete Laplacian spatial filtering on lightness $L^*$ at pore scale (0.35 mm via iris scale `pixelsPerMm`); penalizes confidence under camera blur.
  - **Wrinkles (`wrinkles`) [Beta]**: Directional ridge/groove filtering in forehead and periorbital regions; labeled beta with confidence capped at 0.70.
  - **Dark Circles (`darkCircles`) [Beta]**: Relative periorbital contrast and infraorbital shadowing compared against adjacent cheek baseline; labeled beta with confidence capped at 0.75.
- **Scoring Layer & Aggregation (`scoring/aggregate.ts`)**:
  - Computes composite skin index (0–100) weighting core vs beta tiers.
  - Identifies top primary concerns and derives overall confidence from capture quality.

### Phase 4: Acne Detection (Classical v1)
- **Blemish & Acne Estimator (`analyzers/acne.ts`)**:
  - Biological scale normalisation using iris `pixelsPerMm` (lesion radius ~1.2 mm).
  - Multi-scale blob detection on combined erythema and local contrast map.
  - Robust false-positive suppression:
    - Rejects specular highlights from sebum/lighting.
    - Rejects linear structures (eyelashes, hairs) via orthogonal gradient checks.
    - Rejects non-erythematous flat freckles and moles.
    - Rejects texture boundaries.
  - Non-diagnostic cosmetic severity banding ("Minimal", "Mild", "Moderate", "Marked").
  - Per-skin-tone contrast adjustment and confidence scaling for Fitzpatrick IV–VI tones.
- **Worker Integration (`vision.worker.ts`)**:
  - Pre-computes shared CIELAB buffers once for all 7 analyzers, executing complete face analysis in under 40 ms.
- **Visualizer Screen (`/debug/analyzers`)**:
  - Live dev visualizer allowing instant inspection of all 7 analyzers, canonical UV heatmaps, regional scores, and worker latency.
